# Avenue Bank

> Avenue Bank Ltd (ABN 24 628 073 085, AFSL 520239) is an Australian Authorised Deposit-taking Institution (ADI) regulated by APRA, holding a full unrestricted banking licence granted in 2024. Founded by Dale Hurley and Colin Porter (co-founders of CreditorWatch) and majority-backed by Sherman Ma's Liberty Financial Group, Avenue is a digital business bank specialising exclusively in bank guarantees for SMEs, alongside term deposits. As an ADI it is a designated data holder under Australia's Consumer Data Right (CDR / Open Banking), though it publishes no public developer portal and its unauthenticated Product Reference Data (PRD) API surface is not yet confirmed live.

## APIs

- [Avenue Bank CDR Product Reference Data API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#get-products): UNVERIFIED. The standard CDR Product Reference Data (PRD) endpoint every active Australian ADI data holder must expose at /cds-au/v1/banking/products. Avenue's API host (https://api.avenuebank.com.au/cds-au/v1/banking/products) returned HTTP 403 (WAF) as of 2026-07-20 and Avenue is not yet listed in the CDR Register's banking data-holder brands. When stood up, the contract conforms to the shared DSB Consumer Data Standards OpenAPI, not an Avenue-proprietary spec.
